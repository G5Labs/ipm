Method. I ran `python3 checks/framework.py models/<f>.py` for temp_repo, attr_evaluator, semver, history_display and owners. All five are as-built MATCH. The five runs started in the same second, so `results/checks/20261114T033709Z.json` holds only the last family. I then read the variant code, the rejecting field and the raw log line behind that field.

Paths: product files are relative to `src/cls/IPM/`. Twins are `models/*.py`. Runs are `native/results/<run>/raw.log`, shortened to the run's suffix.

## Agreement

**13 of 16 credits hold** (10 agree-credit, 3 credit-but-weak). **3 of 16 do not.**

Weak but counted: SkipCreate (the TR4 branch is shown only by ID drift, and its variant ignores the unique RootIndex; the credit rests on the missing-folder variant), SetHistoryLastPurge (only the marker write is observed, not the skip), DisplayQueryResult (only a cosmetic header variant).

## Disagreements

1. **Storage/Module.cls::WriteAfterInstallMessage** fails rule part (2) and the consequentiality condition. It is a guarded write of `%Evaluate`'s result. Its variants test the forward to `%Evaluate` (the same P1 field already credits `%Evaluate`), an exception passing through, and the caller's placement after the commit (Utils/Module.cls:1347-1354). The consequential outcome belongs to LoadNewModule and EvaluateMacroHelper.
2. **Utils/LockManager.cls::%OnNew** fails rule part (4), and so part (3). The only rejecting case, LK5, never ran: `TSTART` gave `<UNIMPLEMENTED>` (r4-owners raw.log:161-163), so there was no outer transaction and no BuildCache call. `build=1` is stale, `tlevel=0`, and `lockable=1` is trivially true. The twin's `tlevel=0` matches the failed run, not the modeled case.
3. **Repo/Filesystem/Definition.cls::ScanDirectory** does not demonstrate rule parts (3) and (4). The single rejecting field, `D.setup buildcache=1`, depends on the untested premise that FileSet lists the dot-directory `.modules`. The run has no control showing that the depth cut-off ran or that ignoring it fails.

One side finding: LK5 also backs no other claim in the owners family. Any obligation or member that cites "LK5" as evidence of behavior inside an outer transaction needs a rerun in which the transaction actually starts, for example with TSTART inside a routine or method rather than in direct mode.
