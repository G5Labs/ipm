# Round 2: resolver, versions, update, uninstall and publish selection

## The two questions

**What consequential outcome can IPM distinguish that our model cannot?** Which version of a dependency a requirement
admits. P's resolver proof takes the version algebra as exact set arithmetic (trust item D2) and models versions as
integers (D10); G does not model versions. IPM's own range parser (`General/SemanticVersionExpression/Range.cls`)
decides what `<2`, `>=1.2`, `^1.2` or a combination of two requirements mean, and neither arm checks it.

**What wrong outcome could our checker still accept?** P's model and P's ontology (DEP-D2 uses `>=2.0` as an example)
accept "a dependency declared `<2` installs a 1.x version" and "combining requirements does not depend on order". Both
are wrong for IPM as built.
