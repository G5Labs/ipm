# IPM ontology organization

The owner asked for a clearer distinction between shared concepts and features, natural sections with independently readable commitments, and prose that follows the G5 harness and example ontology. The owner suggested data, security and testing policies, invariants, operations and deployment as possible subjects and left the choice of organization open.

The owner clarified that the change is organizational and must preserve the semantics of the original ontology blocks. The [design proposal](../../pr_ontology_diff_design/ontology-organization/ontology-plan.md) records the boundaries and the comparison with the original uplift.
