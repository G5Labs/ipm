# Provider premises of the B1 model (IPM load and history)

Each premise is a claim about IRIS (or about IRIS behaviour that IPM code relies on) that the L2 model in `crates/ipm-load-history/src/model.rs` encodes. A claim that depends on a premise is conditional until the premise is confirmed natively. Native verdicts come from B2 (`../uplift/ipm-verus-20261113/B/native-report.md` section 2, runs under `results/native/`). B2 IDs are N-P01..N-P18; A2 IDs (`A/operation-inventory.md` section 0) are P-1..P-10.

Status values: CONFIRMED (native), CONFIRMED-DOC (IRIS documentation, not tested natively), PARTIAL, UNVERIFIED, REFUTED (the model was changed to follow the native result).

## Consequences for the claims

- Theorems R1, R2, R3-top, COMMIT-SOUND, TOP-RECORD-COMPLETE, TLEVEL and PERSIST-TARGET are proved about the model. Their product meaning is conditional on P-01, P-02, P-04, P-05, P-06a, P-07 and P-08, all CONFIRMED.
- Terminate-like predictions (cases `...T`) are conditional on P-06b and P-06c (both CONFIRMED).
- NoJournal predictions are conditional on P-03 and P-09 (both CONFIRMED).
- Predictions about `resources_loaded` after a rollback rest on P-10 (CONFIRMED for classes).
