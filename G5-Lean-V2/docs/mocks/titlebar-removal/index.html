Title Bar Removal

The 44px workspace title bar goes away under the alignment layout. Its controls move into the ribbon row's right end; workspace discovery and fork move to Home and to the "+" tab. Pick a screen and poke the controls.

What moves where

  • Title bar removed under the alignment layout (desktop). The tab strip stays as the top edge.
  • Two variants. A: everything from the old bar joins the ribbon's right end. B (chosen 2026-10-11): the five global controls (Workers, Notifications, Report, Settings, Account) sit at the right end of the tab strip as small icons, on every page, while Owner, Start/Stop and workspace Details stay in the ribbon. B keeps the ribbon shorter and gives Home and the "+" page the same global icons without a header row of their own.
  • Right cluster (variant A) appended to the ribbon row after the last group, right-aligned, in the ribbon's own button format (22px glyph, label under it): owner, Start/Stop, then Workers, Notifications, Report, Details, Settings, Account. Chat toggle joins it only when the tab bar setting is off. Base shows the cluster alone, since Base has no ribbon.
  • Owner + Transfer become one control: the user-with-key icon labelled "Owner: name"; for people who may transfer, it is a dropdown listing the other org members, click a name to transfer (with an inline confirm).
  • VM pill + Start/Stop become one button whose label follows the status: Start (stopped, failed), Stop (running), Starting… / Waiting… / Stopping… (disabled, spinner). The status colour is a dot on the button; the full status text is the tooltip. Owner only, as today.
  • Workspace switcher and Fork leave the workspace page. Home gets a search box over the full workspace tree with Open / Fork per row and a "New workspace" button; the "+" fork picker gets the same search over every organization.
  • Activity button removed (the rail's Activity row already opens it). Home button stays removed (the strip owns it).

Assumptions to confirm

  • Primary issue chip and collaborator avatars ("2 online") are dropped with the bar (decided 2026-10-11). The rail's Issues section still lists the primary issue.
  • Home's own top bar (G5 · Workspaces dropdown · bug · account) is removed too for consistency; its controls land in Home's header row. If Home should keep its bar, only the search box changes.
  • Cluster style follows the person's "Action bar" setting like every other ribbon button: icon-only under minimal-icons, text buttons under extended-text. The mock shows the default extended-icons.
  • Other layouts (classic, both, simple) keep the title bar unchanged; this change is alignment-only. Mobile keeps its own header.
  • Terminal button is dropped from the bar (decided 2026-10-11); the Terminal center tab stays reachable from the rail and chat.

Interactions in this mock

  • Click the owner control to open Transfer ownership; pick a name, confirm. Switch Viewer to see the read-only owner badge and the hidden VM button.
  • Change VM status to see the button relabel. Click Start/Stop to simulate the transition.
  • Type in Home's or the "+" page's search box to filter the lineage tree; matches highlight, parents stay visible.
  • "Before" shows the current bar with the removed region outlined.
