How to vote.
  1. Pick an option in a card (or write a line in the text box).
  2. Press Vote in the card. That one press records your vote: your name appears under the option at once. You can change or withdraw your vote any time.
A vote is not a decision: the workspace owner’s Answer is. The owner sees the tally in the same cards.

2 · Workspace tabs: one mounted page or many rooms

PR #380’s tab strip navigates within one mounted workspace page: switching tabs changes the URL, tears the page down and rebuilds it from what was saved. The UXv2 mock kept every open workspace alive in its own iframe, like a hotel where every room stays lit.

ThingAfter switching away and back (PR #380)Why
Which workspace is openkeptIt is the URL; the tab strip only navigates.
Which documents and panels were open in the centerrestoredSaved per workspace in the browser; restored on every switch.
Phase, approvals, timeline, documents, chat historyreloadedDurable server records; the page fetches them again.
Unsent text in the chat boxlostLives only in the running page.
Scroll position, an open dialog, a half-filled formlostRunning-page state; the page is rebuilt.
Live connection to the workspacereopenedOne connection at a time; nothing on the server stops.

The open decision: is per-workspace restore of the open center tabs enough, or do you want running state preserved too? Saving the draft and the scroll position per workspace is a small follow-up; keeping whole pages mounted would need the workspace page to be made safe to run several times at once, a separate and larger piece of work.

3 · Where the UX v2 plan contradicts the prompts

56 prompts from the UXv2 mock session (Sep 7 to Oct 9) and the mock’s change log were compared against the UX v2 plan. The plan was written from the Oct 6 UI meeting, which cut the mock down to a V1; most things missing from the plan are deliberate deferrals. But in six places the plan says the opposite of what a prompt asked, and five asked-for items are dropped without the promised V2 label.

C5 · Activity is the primary issue’s page, with a composer

Needs a decision
What you asked
“The activity log of the primary one is the same as the activity log for the workspace. When clicking on activity log it essentially shows the primary issue page.”prompt #26 · 2026-09-27
Composer “Posting as <person> to <issue> · <tracker>”; tracker comments and events interleaved.change log W13
What the plan says
Status header with the primary issue title/link and purpose; “The feed references, never duplicates, issue discussion.” No tracker comments, no composer.ontology-plan.md:46, 144–146 · Phase 1

Why it matters: You want one place that is both the workspace log and the issue conversation. The plan builds a platform-event feed that links out to the issue. Both can be right, but they are different pages.

C6 · Deployment plan with rollback knobs vs “IE ends at merge”

Wording
What you asked
