No application, test, migration, or service was executed for this adjudication; no application, ontology, or skill was edited.

Source roots: **F** = `/private/tmp/g5-balance-20260928/r-n` (frozen Experiment 7 application); **I** = `/private/tmp/g5-balance-20260928/r-i-o` (frozen implementation `844536f`). An inherited defect remains consequential when the feature composes the faulty operation; inheritance is recorded separately from whether the resulting behavior violates the contract. ## Additional admission adjudication

A valid future-deadline capacity nudge should permit a fresh retry submission when the remaining ordinary authority, health and capacity checks pass. [v9 nudge contract](/private/tmp/g5-balance-20260928/r-i-o/docs/lean-backend-design-v9.md:445) expressly keeps running admission open; the [real nudge writer](/private/tmp/g5-balance-20260928/r-i-o/backend/g5-api-core/src/services/workspace_vm_service.rs:2784) persists a stop identity and pending reason without changing running status. I-O [retry admission](/private/tmp/g5-balance-20260928/r-i-o/backend/g5-api-core/src/services/job_service/retry.rs:466) refuses any pending reason or stop identity without examining its deadline. I-N's frozen initial patch (`implementations/i-n/initial.patch`) likewise requires both fields null. This is **over-restrictive contract behavior**, not evidence of safer ordinary admission. Actual stopping, expired/malformed nudge state, transfer or unhealthy custody must still refuse. No nudge runtime case was executed here.

I-O's delivered [mutation-policy check](/private/tmp/g5-balance-20260928/r-i-o/backend/g5-api-core/src/services/job_service/retry.rs:437) and [host-health checks](/private/tmp/g5-balance-20260928/r-i-o/backend/g5-api-core/src/services/job_service/retry.rs:444) are present. Draft-stage omissions repaired by its own native checks are not delivered defects. New guard proposals should be evaluated against allowed controls as well as prohibited states.

strengthen those source findings; the strengthened nudge probe invokes the lifecycle owner, while

R-I-O's final evidence paragraph understates the supplied test source: its large
integration test contains nonterminal-source, mixed-failed-prerequisite, missing-input
and changed-request refusals, not only a happy path. Reading those tests does not prove
they passed, but absent execution evidence must not be misreported as absent test cases.
The review remains useful; this specific coverage claim needs correction.

## What the comparisons support

Initial N/O/H/P all missed the two pre-established targets and none falsely blocked R3/R4. Their additional findings differ: N exposes the pending-fork composition error, O the Stop/Start fence, H the preadmission Cancel race, and P accepted-launch drift. Several are confirmed despite being absent from the original ledger; P's launch drift matters even though its raw-key and delayed-event arguments need qualification. The matched current/prior skill pair therefore does not establish current-harness superiority.

For I-O, both independent reviewers find launch drift and UI replay recovery; N adds sync projection loss, while O adds the job-cap and ownership issues. That is useful complementary inspection, not evidence that either arm finds a complete defect universe. Missing test/browser/provider coverage is an evidence limit, not automatically a code defect. Directed follow-ups, source proofs, supplied runtime probes, architectural conformity, and open product contracts remain separate observations. No scalar grade or synthetic precision/recall is assigned.
