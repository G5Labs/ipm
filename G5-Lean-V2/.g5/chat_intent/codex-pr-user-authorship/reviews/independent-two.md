## Finding

### P2 — Preserve the inner destination when login interrupts the shared connection route

Location: `frontend/src/lib/githubConnection.ts:11-12`, reached from `frontend/src/App.tsx:294-296` and `frontend/src/components/settings/GithubConnectionCard.tsx:20-23`.

A user with an expired/missing G5 session can open `/account/github?returnTo=%2Facme%2Fwork%3Fview%3Dactivity` (the new recovery link or a bookmark). The new route is protected by RequireAuth (`App.tsx:845-852`), which saves that exact account route in the post-login redirect (`hooks/useAuth.tsx:647-652`). After login, LoginPage passes the preserved route into GithubConnectionCard. The sanitizer discards any `/account/github` path without extracting its safe `returnTo`, converting the destination to `/`. Both the skip link and subsequent OAuth callback consequently lose `/acme/work?view=activity`. An already connected user is also sent directly to `/`. In this app, `/` leads to LandingRedirect and `/coming-soon.html` (`App.tsx:774-777,871`), so recovery exits the authenticated workflow rather than returning to the intended workspace.

Governing rule: `g5.settings.github-connection` and the central-connection amendment require preserving the intended destination and a usable optional continuation after login.

Suggested fix: distinguish the preserved account-management route from a recursively supplied OAuth destination. Resume the authenticated account page directly when login was initiated there, or safely extract and validate its inner returnTo before building the optional offer. Keep recursive/unsafe destinations rejected. Choose the authenticated default destination for invalid input rather than relying on the public `/` route where appropriate.

Regression: exercise `/account/github?returnTo=<workspace query>` through unauthenticated RequireAuth and login, for connected and disconnected grants. Verify skip and OAuth success both retain the intended workspace, and management/reconnect remains reachable if that was the original destination.

Evidence: isolated Vitest probe in `/private/tmp/g5-pr-auth-review-two/return.test.tsx` rendered the real App at /login after establishing the exact saved destination produced by RequireAuth. Expected skip href `/acme/work?view=activity`; actual href `/`. This is a rendered-component regression, not a complete external OAuth/browser run. The isolated probe failed for this assertion only. An earlier probe invocation failed before collection because of the /tmp symlink/root configuration; corrected config then executed successfully and exposed the defect.
