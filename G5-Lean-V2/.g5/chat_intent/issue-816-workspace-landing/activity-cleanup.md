The owner requested an audit of repeated landing/Activity text and a simpler filter
where Tasks can be selected in one click from All. They requested review mocks and
a detailed Project Doc and explicitly excluded removal of surrounding page controls.

The owner approved implementation of the published Current/Proposed mocks and
specification on October 25, 2026. Start, Merge to parent, Guided, the toolbar and
surrounding navigation remain outside scope. “Selected: All” and “Proposed
interaction: All → Tasks → Tasks + Approvals → All” are mock explanations only;
the implementation must add no redundant text beyond the full-page proposed mock.

The accepted design and its scope are in the existing
[landing plan](../../pr_ontology_diff_design/issue-816-workspace-landing/ontology-plan.md#activity-cleanup-and-filter-proposal--october-25-follow-up).
