Rules it satisfies

  • 1.3.1 / 4.1.2 — list and headings are real; status words are text, the circles are aria-hidden.
  • 1.4.1 — done, next and to do are never colour alone.
  • 1.4.11 — ring stroke white 35% on bg1 is 3.3:1; focus ring 7.5:1.
  • 2.4.3 / 2.4.7 / 2.4.11 — five logical stops; visible focus; not obscured by the sticky ribbon.
  • 2.5.8 — every target 24px or more; the ring's hit area is 40px.
  • 4.1.3 — mark changes and job completions are announced politely; errors use role="alert".
  • Reduced motion — no spinner loop; “Running” is a word.
