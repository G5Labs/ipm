Mock R — motion spec

Every transition the panel is allowed, with its tier, choreography and reduced-motion fallback, playable at the real width. Rules applied: things done tens of times a day get 120ms or nothing; occasional changes get 180ms; the once-per-workspace moment gets 240ms and one soft entrance. Transform and opacity only, except the accordion's height. Transitions, never keyframes, for anything a person can toggle twice a second, so Undo retargets mid-flight. Nothing travels more than 20% of the panel width. Loops stop under reduced motion; colour and opacity changes stay.
