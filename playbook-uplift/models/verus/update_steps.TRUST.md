## Proof layout and cost

Per playbook 3.3: every L2 step is a `#[verifier::opaque]` function, the dispatcher lemmas (`inst_refines`,
`dep_refines`) call one lemma per step and each reveals only its own step. Quantifiers carry explicit triggers;
triggers on `Map` indexing and on the recursive `sched_upto` did not fire across struct copies in this Verus version
and were replaced by `contains_key` triggers and named predicates (`elem_ok`, `log_is`). Verus
0.2026.09.27.3cf1832, `--rlimit 30`.
