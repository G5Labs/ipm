## When an iterative loop is requested

Use one master agent to run the loop, with fresh workers for substantive batches and fresh reviewers at integrated checkpoints. This orchestration and the [iteration](uplift-iteration.md) and [scoring](uplift-scoring.md) prompts apply to both TLC and Verus. Each backend keeps its modeling, execution and evidence rules. An ordinary uplift or maintenance request does not start a continuous loop.

1. **Establish the run.** Retain the product revision, starting foundation, source inventory, scope, published scores, permissions and requested model/effort. Carry forward the reconciled [obligation census](uplift-scoring.md#census-of-ontology-obligations), or establish it at the starting checkpoint. Persist the product-wide queue, assignments, completed checkpoints, review counter and costs in the existing plan. Honor the user's iteration, time and cost limits and stop instructions. An explicit request to continue until convergence or stop replaces the initial uplift's default three-review-round cap for that run; a generic request to iterate does not. Do not reset a limit when creating a worker or resuming.
2. **Assign a coherent batch to a fresh worker.** Use the [iteration prompt](uplift-iteration.md) to select a consequential gap from the current obligation census or a workflow discovered independently from product sources. Group related decisions that share a fixture, owner or reader when useful. Give the worker the current checkpoint, relevant census entries, sources, owners, models, published feedback, write scope, permitted execution and expected handoff. Reference durable evidence instead of copying the entire conversation. Ask it to complete the batch and return residual work; the master owns the next assignment and overall convergence. If fresh isolation is unavailable, report the limitation and preserve useful progress without calling same-context review independent.
3. **Integrate and validate.** Inspect the returned artifacts and resolve overlaps. Recheck affected claims, valid neighbors and controls after the final relevant edit, or establish permitted evidence reuse. Keep partial work recoverable, with its missing steps explicit; a commit or successful process exit is not a completed check. Reconcile canonical ontology, model-to-code links, findings, simplifications and questions under the selected foundation guide.
4. **Recount and choose again.** Use the same counting rule on each checkpoint's current evidence. Update the queue from the integrated artifacts, preserving residual workflows inside a completed area. Report material predictions or source roles gained, corrected or withdrawn and their cost. Cheap inventory recounts can run each batch; use independent semantic scoring at meaningful comparison checkpoints. The evaluator keeps undisclosed cases private. A saturated old panel does not replace fresh source-selected coverage checks.
5. **Review the integrated foundation.** At substantial checkpoints and before declaring convergence, use fresh reviewers following [independent review and repair](uplift-review.md). Review coverage and fidelity beyond the last finding list. Feed adjudicated gaps into the same queue, validate repairs and preserve the review count across workers. Review scheduling must respect the run's limits and backend requirements.
6. **Let the master decide whether to stop.** Continue dispatching while material tractable work remains within the requested limits. A worker finishing its area, fewer new bugs or a stable score is not product-wide convergence. Apply the iteration prompt's cross-area challenge before claiming scoped convergence, supported by independent review. Otherwise stop only at the requested limit, on the user's instruction, or when a major blocker prevents meaningful progress; report which condition ended the run and preserve unfinished work.

Fresh sessions give workers a bounded context and add context-loading and review cost. Judge their value by supported coverage and precision gains alongside total worker, reviewer, orchestration and checker costs. More iterations, links, proofs or findings alone do not establish efficiency. For a requested comparison of loop strategies, use [controlled harness evaluation](uplift-evaluation.md); ordinary progress reports are not a matched experiment.
