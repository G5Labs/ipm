# Score an uplift — evaluator prompt

Independently compare the named foundation snapshots on the same pinned product. The two questions are: can a reader predict consequential behavior from its owning statements, and can the formal foundation distinguish it and identify the code responsible? Start from the scope and any previous **published** score and feedback. Retain the underlying identities and evidence, not just totals.

## Shared units, backend-specific evidence

TLC and Verus use the same source-supported decisions, neighboring inputs or histories, observable outcomes, ontology owners and source-member census. Score the semantic obligation, not the number of models, explored states or verified proof units. Preserve the strength and scope of the evidence beside the score; equal coverage scores do not imply equivalent guarantees.

| Backend | Evidence required for the claimed result |
| --- | --- |
| TLA+/TLC | Read the specification and actual configuration together: initialization, enabled actions, finite domains, constraints, reductions and checked properties. Record completion, witnesses/counterexamples and controls, separating safety, deadlock, reachability and liveness with its fairness assumptions. A completed finite-instance check is not an unbounded proof or verification of production code. Apply [TLC correspondence and model review](TLC/model-checking.md) and [execution evidence](TLC/setup_foundation.md#execution-evidence-and-reuse). |
| Verus | Read the exact spec, theorem or executable-body contract, quantified domain, preconditions, dependencies and trusted boundaries. Require the recorded proof result and meaningful controls. If exploration is used, retain its domains, actions, bounds, completion and correspondence to the proved definitions. A transcribed model proof is distinct from a proof of the product's executable body. Apply [Verus verification](Verus/verification.md) and [execution evidence](Verus/setup_foundation.md#execution-evidence-and-reuse). |

A safety invariant cannot substitute for required progress, and finding one recovery path cannot establish recovery on every fair execution. An expected counterexample can establish an as-built divergence within its scope; a timeout, parser failure or failed proof alone cannot. For either backend, inspect actual source correspondence and retain the missing boundary when only a runtime probe distinguishes the behavior. If compared models use different domains or observations, report the common-scope score and each additional scope separately.

## Score and diagnose the foundation

| Headline score | Count on each snapshot |
| --- | --- |
| Ontology coverage | Fully modeled obligations / all applicable obligations in the pinned ontology. Give the counts and percentage for each owning block and for the whole foundation. Use the census below. |
| Ontology precision | Fully supported predictions / answered questions. Also show supported / scheduled, partial, wrong and unconfirmed; an unanswered question is charged to coverage. Report as-built agreement separately from intended policy and human approval. |
| Formal model coverage | Decisions for which the actual specification represents both reachable neighbors and the relevant observation across the necessary boundary / scheduled decisions. Prose, Python, an uninterpreted result parameter or a proof of only the caller does not supply the producer rule. Identify what was proved, bounded, refuted or left unchecked separately. |
| Formal model precision | Fully supported predictions / answered questions, also supported / scheduled, partial, wrong and unconfirmed. Read the property and premises; check the real producer and reader or qualify the inference. Count a supported abstraction only within its stated domain. |
| Model-to-code link reach | Distinct in-scope named production members with a current, resolvable model-to-source citation or binding / **all** in-scope named production members. Take the union across evidence formats on the same pin and count each member once. Beside this one percentage, give the count with a registered active behavioral binding as a subset. Pin roots, unit, identities, exclusions and counting script. Exclude ontology-only and test-only citations, stale, unresolved, ambiguous, file-only and out-of-census references from the numerator. Recount both snapshots under the same pinned rule before reporting a gain, reading each snapshot’s current citations as well as its bindings; do not freeze one evidence format at the baseline. If a common frame cannot be reconstructed, give each historical count with its definition. |

### Census of ontology obligations

At each scored seal, reconcile every canonical intent and design block (or its equivalent in that foundation) to the existing coverage map. Read the block’s own lead, bullets, prose, tables and exceptions. Split claims when one outcome can fail while another still holds. Keep conditions with the outcome they qualify. Count a restatement once at its owner and trace aliases or moved text to that entry. A single model may cover several obligations, and several models may jointly cover one. Record purely structural text, rationale and undecided alternatives with their reason for exclusion. A stated working assumption about current behavior is assessable within its declared scope. A difficult promise stays in scope.

For a parent, identify what it promises beyond its children. A pure heading or restatement with no additional obligation can be marked structural after review. For a distinct parent promise, identify the child properties and show why their guarantees together entail it under compatible assumptions. Credit each entailed parent obligation once and keep any residual obligation in that block’s denominator. A guarantee about order, shared state or completion across children can still be missing when each child passes.

For each obligation record a stable identity, owner and text locator, distinguishing cases and observable result, model/property locator(s), domain and evidence. Grade it **full, partial, absent or unassessed**. Full requires the formal rule to distinguish the required cases through the stated observation, directly or by documented composition. Record whether the rule was proved, checked within bounds, refuted or unchecked, with the receipt or evidence gap. A modeled counterexample can refute a design promise. A passing badge, link, proposed repair or runtime-only check cannot by itself establish full representation of the as-built behavior.

For a complete inventory report `full / (full + partial + absent + unassessed)` per block and pooled across blocks, alongside the four counts. Beside it show how many applicable obligations have a passing design check over their full stated scope, with bounded results and known violations identified. With unassessed entries, label model coverage a confirmed lower bound and give the upper bound `(full + unassessed) / total`. Mark an empty block denominator N/A. If enumeration is incomplete, report the assessed scope and leave the whole-foundation percentage unmeasured. On a later seal reuse an adjudication only while its statement, model, premises and evidence still apply. Reconcile additions, splits, merges, deletions and losses. Show change on retained obligations separately from change in scope so deleting an uncovered promise cannot appear as a repair.

Keep the following source-selected draws for discovering behavior missing from the ontology and for testing precision. Report how many drawn decisions the owner states completely as a panel diagnostic. Feed consequential census gaps and source-discovered omissions into the published feedback queue.

Take claimed links from the project’s existing coverage and correspondence map ([TLC](TLC/uplift.md#b-model-behavior-and-connect-it-to-implementation), [Verus](Verus/setup_foundation.md#where-to-find-every-model-to-code-link)). Compare it with the independent production member census, resolve links in both directions and identify members with no locator and members with only locators by subsystem. If independent review finds an active link missing from the map, report the omission so the next iteration can reconcile it. Prefer a deterministic repository-native check for membership, resolution, freshness and the deduplicated numerator. On selected workflows independently enumerate the necessary deciding, writing and observing roles. Check which registered links actually constrain their claimed role. Report supported / reviewed bindings and bound / expected roles. When reach rises and decision scores stay flat or fall, inspect the additions as well as the missing roles (all additions if inexpensive, otherwise a declared stratified sample). Additions are the union of newly reached members and members gaining an active binding. Give each count, overlap and distinct total, and disclose losses. Explain new locators, newly bound inherited behavior, new formal distinctions, and correspondence confirmed or withdrawn at the reader. On the same source-selected contrast identify the first absent or contradicted step in the owner, formal rule or implementation evidence. On a reviewed workflow, try to construct two results at the real reader that differ in the outcome under review and both satisfy the formal guarantee for the same relevant inputs. If the canonical owner or product evidence rules either out, do not count the formal claim as fully supported; put the missing distinction in the feedback queue. If both are valid outcomes, record that scope; the claim stands within it. A resolved declaration is a locator; it does not prove fidelity or the whole method. For registered active members, distinguish direct executable-body verification, transcription with native agreement, inspection only and unassessed support. Where possible report independently supported material bindings / independently assessed material members, counting each member once within the selected scope. Keep excluded, trivial, unassessed and blocked work visible; do not silently shrink the raw denominator.

For a running loop, the **evaluator** opens a prospective comparison window. Before the measured work starts, freeze the product pin, eligible before/after seal rule (including early convergence), sampled identities, expected observable neighbors and exclusions in an evaluator-only ledger. Publish its timestamped digest and counting rules. Builders can use the public rules immediately. Keep cases, locators, expected answers and draw seeds private until the after seal. Grade both seals on the same frozen cases. An after-the-fact sample can support a retrospective comparison. Only cases with credible independence can support a claim about unseen discovery.

Use two complementary draws. From pinned product sources, draw cross-module workflows across subsystems, including poorly bound areas and already-linked paths where a different final reader could change the result. Separately sample the **members without an active behavioral binding at baseline**, including locator-only members. Classify each sampled member from product evidence as consequential, shared/delegating, trivial for the assessed behavior, or unresolved. For consequential cases record whether each seal binds the relevant decision, effect and observable reader, and whether its model determines the contrast. Report those counts alongside raw reach. Keep the original sample identities when links change, and report unresolved, ineligible and invalid cases explicitly. Record the frame, strata, seed or selection rationale, sample sizes and known exposure. Separate disclosed repairs, independent discovery, high-impact challenges and builder-selected work. Show the paired sensitivity if an oracle fails. A purposive panel cannot estimate a project-wide rate. Human salience or comprehension without owner evidence remains unmeasured.

Report the five scores with denominators, gains, losses, withdrawals, wrong answers, and budget/cost (builder and grader separate; elapsed, solver time, tokens/cache, currency if known). Alongside scores report genuinely new versus strengthened/withdrawn issues, simplifications proposed/tested/retired and questions opened/resolved/still open. Match by trigger and observable consequence, not ID count; explain who or what settled an item. These counts have no preferred direction.

Return replayable ledgers and a short prioritized feedback queue. For each material gap give the source workflow or members, missing decision/role, current owner or absence, what the formal claim fails to distinguish, and the contrasting observation that would establish closure. Recheck meaningful passing neighbors and failing controls; disclose what was carried forward. Release repair cases only after the after-seal. For the next comparison, keep a disjoint reserve private or freeze a fresh independent draw before work starts. If seeded, use a separate private seed for that draw and publish only a digest of the private ledger. Treat cases identifiable from earlier prompts, audits or examples as exposed, including overlaps at the level of the decision and reader. Grade subsequent work on those cases as repair and record uncertain exposure. If an independent evaluator or representative evidence is unavailable, label the result accordingly.
